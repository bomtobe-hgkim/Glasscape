## English

### Application data stays on your computer

Glasscape is a Windows desktop personalization app. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or telemetry that sends your activity to the publisher.

Videos you select are played from your computer. Glasscape stores file paths, titles, optional local thumbnail images, display choices, and playback/application preferences to restore your settings. Error logs remain in your Windows user profile and may contain file paths and technical error details. The app does not upload your videos, thumbnails, settings or logs to the publisher.

### Windows integration

To manage desktop effects, Glasscape checks connected displays, the foreground window's screen bounds, Windows taskbar state, battery/power state, and session lock/sleep state. This information is used locally for taskbar and wallpaper behavior. The app does not provide messaging, a social feed, purchases or cloud storage.

Removing a video from the library removes its library entry; the original video remains on your computer. You can change settings in the app and manage its local data using Windows application-data controls. Microsoft Store installation, delivery, updates and diagnostics are governed by Microsoft's policies.

### Support and public repository

If you email **bomtobe.sw@gmail.com**, the publisher receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, API keys or other secrets. Diagnostic files are not sent automatically; share only the information you intend to disclose. You may use the same email address for questions about support information or to request its deletion.

Issues and comments you post in the public GitHub repository are public and are handled by GitHub under its policies. Use email for private support or security reports.

### Changes and contact

Updates to this policy are published in this file with an updated effective date. Contact **bomtobe.sw@gmail.com** with privacy questions.

라이브러리에서 동영상을 제거해도 원본 파일은 컴퓨터에 남습니다. 앱과 Windows의 앱 데이터 관리 기능을 통해 설정을 변경하거나 로컬 데이터를 관리할 수 있습니다. Microsoft Store의 설치, 배포, 업데이트와 진단 서비스에는 Microsoft의 정책이 적용됩니다.
